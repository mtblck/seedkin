# Seedkin name and logo use

The Seedkin name and logos identify Seedkin and the people who make it. The code in this project is open source; the brand is not (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This page says what you may do with the name and logos without asking.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to talk about Seedkin: in an article, a review, a talk, a tutorial or a list of tools.
- Say in plain words that your project works with, is built on, or is a plugin or integration for Seedkin, as long as it is clear that Matt Black did not make it.
- Use the unmodified logo to link to or point at Seedkin, following the brand guidelines (clear space, colours, minimum size).
- Keep these files unchanged in a copy or fork of the project, as long as the fork does not present itself as Seedkin (below).

- Draw any avatar with the package, jolly-sprocket-65 included, and use it under the MIT license, as your avatar or anywhere else. The rules below are about the Seedkin logo and name, not the avatars.

## You may not

- Release, host or sell a fork, product or service under the Seedkin name or logo, or under a name or logo close enough to be mistaken for it. A fork that others use needs its own name and logo.
- Change the logos: redraw, stretch, recolour outside the guidelines, add to them, or combine them with your own marks.
- Use the name or logos in a way that suggests Matt Black made, sponsors or endorses your work when they don't, or use the Seedkin logo (the mark on its tile, or a lockup with the wordmark) as your own app icon or domain name.

## Anything else

Ask first: https://seedkin.dev. Merchandise, events and anything not listed above need permission.
